Apple Health → Garmin

Turn your Apple Health export into Garmin-importable CSV files — one per year — for Garmin Connect's Import Fitbit Data feature.

🔒 100% in your browser. Your health data never leaves your device — no upload, no server, no storage.

⬆

Drop your export.zip here or click to choose

Accepts the Apple Health export.zip or an extracted export.xml. Big files are fine — they're streamed, not uploaded.

Exclude date ranges (optional)

By default every day is converted. Add ranges here to leave dates out — e.g. days a Garmin device already recorded, or data you don't want to import.

    Processing…

    📱 Step 1 — Export your data from Apple Health
    1. Open the Health app on your iPhone.
    2. Tap the Summary tab, then your profile picture / initials (top-right).
    3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Export All Health Data, then Export.
    4. Wait a few minutes while it prepares the file — the screen may look frozen; leave the app open.
    5. When the Share sheet appears, send it to your computer: Save to Files, AirDrop, etc.
    6. You'll get an export.zip. Drop that file into the box above (no need to unzip it).

    Heads up: the export can take several minutes and is often 100 MB–1 GB+. AirDrop or Save to Files is more reliable than email for big files.

    ⌚ Step 2 — Import the CSVs into Garmin Connect
    1. On a computer, go to connect.garmin.com and sign in.
    2. Check your Garmin account's time zone is correct (Settings → profile) so dates line up.
    3. Click the upload icon (cloud with an up-arrow) at the top-right.
    4. Choose Import Data, then Browse and pick one fitbit_activities_YYYY.csv.
    5. When asked for units, choose Kilometers (the files are metric), then Import Data.
    6. Repeat for each year — one file at a time.
    • One year per file — that's why this tool splits by year.
    • Never open the CSV in Excel first — it reformats the file and Garmin will reject it. Upload it exactly as downloaded.
    • Dates can shift by a day if your Garmin time zone differs from your data's.
    • On days Garmin already has its own data, Garmin's data wins (the import won't overwrite it).
